Informed Consent

Research Study Participation

You are being asked to participate in a research study about collaborative decision-making in engineering design. This study is being conducted by Dr. Paul Grogan and colleagues at Stevens Institute of Technology.

Purpose of the Study

The purpose of this research is to understand how individuals make decisions in collaborative design environments and how information affects these decisions.

What You Will Do

If you agree to participate, you will complete a series of design tasks in collaboration with a partner. The session will include:

  • Demographic survey (approximately 2 minutes)
  • Pre-task questionnaire (approximately 3 minutes)
  • Training tasks to familiarize yourself with the interface (approximately 5 minutes)
  • Main experiment tasks (approximately 30-45 minutes)
  • Post-task questionnaire (approximately 3 minutes)
Risks and Benefits

There are no foreseeable risks to participating in this study. While you may not directly benefit from participation, your contribution will help advance understanding of collaborative design processes.

Confidentiality

Your responses will be kept confidential. All data will be stored securely and will only be accessible to the research team. Your name will not be associated with any publications or presentations resulting from this research.

Voluntary Participation

Your participation is completely voluntary. You may withdraw from the study at any time without penalty by simply closing your browser window.

Study Instructions

Welcome to the Collaborative Design Experiment

In this study, you will be working with a partner to make design decisions. Each task involves choosing between different design options, some of which require collaboration with your partner.

Two-Stage Decision Process

Each task consists of two stages:

  1. Stage 1 - Intention: You will review the available options and indicate your intention toward collaboration on a scale from 1 (definitely will not collaborate) to 10 (definitely will collaborate). The timer will show the first 30 seconds.
  2. Stage 2 - Final Choice: After both you and your partner have submitted your intentions, you will make your final design choice. Any time remaining from the 90-second total will be available for this stage.
Design Options

In each task, you will see:

  • Three Collaborative Options (A, B, C): These require coordination with your partner. The payoff depends on whether your partner also chooses a collaborative option.
  • One Individual Option (Y): This is a safe choice that doesn't depend on your partner's decision.
Timing and Penalties

Important: You have a total of 90 seconds for each task (both stages combined). Time management is crucial:

  • The timer shows 30 seconds during Stage 1 (Intention), then displays remaining time during Stage 2 (Choice)
  • If you finish Stage 1 quickly, more time rolls over to Stage 2
  • No penalties are applied during Stage 1, regardless of time taken
  • After 90 seconds total: You have a 10-second grace period with no penalty
  • After the grace period (100 seconds): A penalty of 1 point per second is deducted from your task score
  • Timer colors: green (normal) → yellow (warning: < 5s left) → red (overtime)
Practice Tasks

Before beginning the main experiment, you will complete 2 practice tasks to familiarize yourself with the interface:

  • Practice Task 1: Extended time (3 minutes) to learn the interface and understand the decision process
  • Practice Task 2: Standard time (90 seconds) to practice the actual timing you'll experience in the main experiment

These practice tasks will not affect your final results.
